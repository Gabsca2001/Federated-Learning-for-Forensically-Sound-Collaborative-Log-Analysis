Different dataset partitions, training operators, input budgets, trigger
families and parent histories must not be pooled as equivalent replications.
Common-input interventions test one-round mechanisms; they do not constitute
separately trained defenses. Full-horizon failure is not replaced by zero ASR.
Historical suite counts apply to their named source releases, not to a newly
claimed omnibus test execution.

## What the recent research established

Authenticity, exact accounting, numerical reconstruction and attack resistance
are distinct properties. Good IID containment does not establish non-IID
containment. A coefficient budget can be mathematically valid yet empirically
unsafe. Rejecting unusual benign clients can remove useful information and
increase the remaining attackers' relative influence. Current-round exclusion
cannot certify recovery of an already contaminated parent. Additional clean
server information has its own provenance, representation and objective costs.

## Completed bounded diagnostic and next decision

The completed bounded diagnosis quantifies the server reference's loss objective
and its alignment with honest client gradients and historical Adam endpoints. A
balanced root with inherited global weights does not necessarily represent
the original objective. Client-local weighted-mean normalization can also make
the sample-weighted mixture differ from the pooled weighted loss. Full-data
oracle quantities are simulation diagnostics, not operational access rights.

The prospective clean gate required ten positive contributors and no more than
two F1 points lost relative to both robust and capped same-input controls for
every clean context and fixed root draw. Only an actual small-root configuration
may qualify; oracle references and norm-controlled diagnostics cannot authorize
a full follow-up. The candidate fails in36/48 context/draw observations. Passing
would have been necessary, not proof of trajectory security.

Correcting class loss mass alone does not fix the transferred operator. The
exact honest-gradient reference still gives fewer than ten positive contributors
in4/16 coherent full-batch-gradient contexts. Historical Adam endpoints and
instantaneous honest descent have mean cosine0.0323 here; the comparison also
differs in local steps, stochasticity and evaluation-mode dropout, so this is
not a causal attribution to Adam alone. The detailed report retains those limits.

A small multiround follow-up requires a separately fixed clean/attacked protocol,
its own baselines, defender-specific attack evaluation and an explicit storage
budget. Failure of the clean gate closed this iteration without another attack
sweep. Any future optimizer/availability-contract change is a separately declared
research revision, not a threshold tweak that converts these failures to success.
The thesis, external witness administration and ToN-IoT remain outside
this consolidation task. No default is changed.
