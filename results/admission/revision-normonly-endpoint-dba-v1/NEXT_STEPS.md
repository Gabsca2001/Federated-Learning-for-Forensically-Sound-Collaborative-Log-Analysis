# Consolidation order after the DBA mechanism ablation

These are pending validation questions, not results or additional requirements
attributed to the professor. Keep the agreed retrospective programme separate
from research on automatic preventive defenses.

1. **Close mechanism attribution first.** Compare unrestricted FedAvg, uniform
   root-norm normalization and cosine-weighted root-norm normalization under
   matched endpoint attacks. The separate six-history ablation addresses this
   step. If both normalized rules contain the measured attacks, do not credit
   direction filtering with a unique empirical benefit. Neither arm is root-free.

2. **Connect the new effective DBA histories to the accepted investigation.**
   Audit existing group analysis and counterfactual code before adding new code.
   For candidate groups selected with clean-only calibration, retain individual
   removals, joint removal and size-matched benign removal controls. Distinguish
   current-round reaggregation from actual corrected-parent retraining in later
   rounds. Report false members, missed attackers, full/component ASR, clean F1
   and parameter residuals. A retrospective group may be compatible with
   coordinated behavior; it is not proof of collusion or authority for permanent
   automatic exclusion. Analyze all retained assignments, not just successes.

3. **Examine distributed-trigger composition explicitly.** Existing construction
   assigns separate components, but some individual components independently
   yield high raw ASR. Do not claim all components are necessary. Compare full
   and individual evaluation triggers, retain those negative results, and use
   individual/joint training interventions to distinguish redundancy from a
   genuinely joint effect. Match malicious identities, activation rounds and
   poisoned-row budgets; label differences in feature-edit and optimizer budgets.
   Any redesigned trigger is a separately frozen protocol, not a replacement for
   the completed histories or a search whose failures disappear from reporting.

4. **Test useful adaptation before operational defense claims.** Freeze a small
   experiment with a less mature starting checkpoint and an adequate horizon.
   Require measurable clean learning as well as ASR containment, quorum and
   false-positive accounting. Compare the same three numerical mechanisms and
   retain unsuccessful or paused histories. Reused-validation warm-start results
   do not establish cold-start learning, root poisoning resistance, arbitrary
   adaptive security or long-term continuity. Only then assess a fresh TPM-bound
   integration with an explicit honest-versus-Byzantine execution contract.

5. **Unify reporting after evidence closes.** Update the property-to-assumption-
   code-verifier-evidence mapping and comparable baseline costs. Preserve the
   earlier failures and the limits of FedGradTrace reproduction. Update thesis
   text only when requested; keep ToN-IoT acquisition and independently managed
   witness deployment deferred under the current instructions.

New runs must use metadata/final-checkpoint retention, no dataset copies, a
64 MiB combined output ceiling per bounded study and an 8 GiB host free-space
floor. No research result silently changes operational defaults.
