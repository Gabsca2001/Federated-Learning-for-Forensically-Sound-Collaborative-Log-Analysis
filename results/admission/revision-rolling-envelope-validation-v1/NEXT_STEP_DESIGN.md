# Next structural experiment — proposal, not an implemented guarantee

The automatic gate should not equate a residual pattern with malicious intent.
Keep mandatory trust rejection and unsupported-context failure. Keep semantic
alerts and retrospective group selection as auditable hypotheses.

A separately specified containment experiment can clip each admitted local
delta to clean-calibrated per-tensor bounds before aggregation, retaining both
the original signed update and the exact derived clipped object. Record clipping
factors, effective masses and the actual aggregate, and independently reconstruct
them. Fit bounds only on reserved clean references, including unmodified local
responses to generic perturbed shared states. Freeze all bounds and the utility
error budget before evaluation; do not fit to these newly observed attack paths.

For a single tensor with convex weights a_i and all clipped deltas of norm at
most C, the aggregate delta has norm at most C. Removing client k and
renormalizing the others changes it by
||a_k(u_k - mu_-k)|| <= 2 a_k C, provided a_k<1. This is a deterministic influence
bound, not proof of benign behavior or a bound on ASR near a decision boundary.
Across later rounds, local updates must be retrained from the modified base;
adding per-round norm bounds does not prove causal recovery or absence of a
backdoor. Quorum, utility loss, non-IID behavior, component triggers, adaptive
search and matched attack-effective controls must all be measured.

This is a concrete next experimental specification. It is not yet code or a
validated mitigation, and clipping already used by a literature baseline must
not be presented as an original defense contribution. The thesis contribution
can instead concern authenticated derivation, exact influence accounting and
reproducible diagnosis under explicit assumptions.
