## Additional research: containment

The supervisor's accepted retrospective-group requirement does not require perfect automatic quarantine. The additional bounded profile has a conditional per-tensor norm and fixed-input removal bound, and has been integrated into an explicit software-TPM runtime profile. Those properties do **not** imply a guarantee on ASR or later retraining. The reserved multi-trigger validation finds strong residual attacks and support/quorum failures; it must be reported as a negative empirical result rather than repaired by tuning on these same cases. It is not promoted to the runtime default.
