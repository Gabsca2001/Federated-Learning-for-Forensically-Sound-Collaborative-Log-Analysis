Reviewed on **9 November 2026**. Completed implementation is recorded in
[STATUS.md](STATUS.md). This plan does not maintain a parallel campaign diary.

## Technical consolidation

1. Preserve profile identity across any future change. The original admission
   policies, transformed aggregation operators and retrospective selectors have
   different contracts and must retain distinct identifiers and evidence.
2. Before operational promotion of V5, define a fresh assessment of successful
   norm-limited/evasive attacks, changed model states, short/staggered episodes
   and benign large updates. Calibration and evaluation assignments must be
   separated before outcomes. The completed study is not silently retuned.
3. Treat continuous-loss utility evidence and state-aware calibration as separate
   proposed extensions. A loss increase alone does not establish maliciousness.
4. Preserve individual, joint and benign intervention controls. Full-origin
   retraining requires retained data and reproducible client training; live
   participant cooperation is a separate deployment question.

These are research directions, not validated implementations or authorization
to launch additional campaigns during a documentation change.

- Named protocol, input boundary, calibration population and intended claim.
- Relevant tests and profile-specific verifier evidence.

- Updated maintained status and result catalogue, without rewriting sealed reports.
