# Correspondence with the accepted supervisor programme

This maintained map combines the accepted formal, receipt and multiround
refinements with later technical evidence. The original correspondence and
[6 November audit](../records/EMAIL_REQUIREMENTS_STATUS_20261106.md) remain dated sources.
The map does not certify every experimental defense as operationally effective.

Automatic quarantine and aggregation containment are additional research paths.
Their failures constrain security claims, but do not erase the implemented
retrospective and formal programme. Conversely, a completed formal verifier does
not establish that an experimental detector identifies every adversary.
