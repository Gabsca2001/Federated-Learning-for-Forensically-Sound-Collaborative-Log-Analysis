## Overall finding

The accepted formal-derivation and retrospective-investigation programme has
been implemented and exercised within its declared premises. It would still
be inaccurate to call the entire email programme complete. The new independent
dataset remains deferred; independent witness administration is absent in the
local deployment; literature reproductions retain adaptations/failures; and
the manuscript does not yet include all later technical developments.

Perfect automatic attacker exclusion was not an accepted email requirement.
The professor asked for retrospective candidate groups, clean-only calibration,
individual/joint interventions and qualified interpretation. Automatic
quarantine, containment and robust aggregation are additional research. Their
negative results limit security claims without erasing the implemented formal
model or the requested retrospective experiments.

## Additional gate work: what changed and what it does not prove

1. The original-policy comparison and clean-only recalibration quantified
   availability, honest-weight and attack problems rather than declaring a
   universal winning gate.
2. The severe-tail profile replaces capped averaging with the maximum of
   uncapped normalized component tails. Its component non-compensation property
   is valid, but it fails the declared research guards: all nine IID attack
   branches have zero complete/component ASR, while non-IID worst ASR remains
   99.97%, and two clean assignments lose excessive nominal mass. All 96 cases,
   including two original-composite quorum failures, remain preserved.
3. The new opt-in mass guard checks retained nominal mass and the largest
   at-most-three coefficient share before calling the numerical backend. It
   never restores rejected mass. Twenty-one focused tests pass; 2,824
   retrospective assessments, 89 actual Flower reconstructions and 1,314
   suppressed backend calls are reconciled independently.
4. The guard exposes an availability incompatibility: with a 1/3 group budget
   it would defer all 45 clean non-IID source rounds. No defended ASR is inferred
   and no operational default is changed. An explicit aggregation/support or
   recovery policy and new reserved validation are needed for deployable
   continuation. A static cap is not the missing empirical proof of robustness.
5. Exact feasibility certificates show that this is more than a bad alarm
   threshold. Under `0<=m_i<=n_i`, retention `r` and any-at-most-three share cap
   `gamma` require every nominal three-client share `c` to satisfy
   `c<=1-r*(1-gamma)`. For `r=4/5,gamma=1/3`, the limit is `7/15`. All three
   non-IID partitions violate it. The current sample-weighted contract cannot
   satisfy both targets even with arbitrary continuous downweights. A separately
   declared final-beta transformation or changed support/requirements is needed;
   no such continuation is validated here. See `FEASIBILITY.md` and the six
   exact certificates.
