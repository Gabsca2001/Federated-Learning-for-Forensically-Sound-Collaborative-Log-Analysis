## Admission-policy reading guide

The four original policy names are defined together in Chapter 4, “The four original admission policies”, with a policy-to-experiment table. TPM-only uses the mandatory M4/M5 trust gate; it is not merely a Quote-signature check or a benign-data guarantee. Statistics-only is a diagnostic ablation. Original sequential means trust followed by a statistical threshold within one round, not temporal accumulation. Gated composite has a hard trust veto followed by the declared combined-risk accept/downweight/quarantine action. Example counts become masses before normalization; quorum belongs to each specific contract.

Repeated live policy experiments compare composite with original sequential. Untargeted adaptive experiments compare composite with TPM-only. Shadow decisions in disagreement experiments do not create alternative trained histories. Later envelope, bounded and robust profiles have separate names and contracts.
